Privacy & customer enquiries

This website prepares an enquiry on your device. It does not store form entries in a website database. Opening WhatsApp shares your prepared message with WhatsApp; you must press Send to contact us.

Messages sent to our business may be retained to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and follow up on your project. If a business assistant is enabled, messages may be processed by our hosting provider and, where configured, Google Gemini to prepare responses. A person confirms final quotes and bookings. Do not send passwords, bank details or sensitive documents in chat.

No analytics or advertising trackers are configured in this version. Vercel hosts the website and may process standard connection logs. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
